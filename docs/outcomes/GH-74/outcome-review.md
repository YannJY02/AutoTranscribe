# GH-74 evidence-linked outcome review

## Decision

- [Inference] **Iterate.** The pilot does not support continuing rollout because its only attempted segment failed and three segments were not observed; stopping the product direction would exceed the evidence. The dry-run validates rollback code paths but does not establish current owner-runtime consent or queue state, so this review cannot decide whether an operational rollback is required. ([Sources: pilot manifest](../../../pilots/gh-73-owner-lifecycle.json), [rollback proof](../../../pilots/evidence/GH-73/rollback-proof.json))
- [Accepted intent] Public release and public portfolio publication remain human approval gates; this repository review is not either approval. ([Sources: GH-74 contract](https://github.com/YannJY02/AutoTranscribe/issues/74), [ADR 0003](../../adr/0003-separate-local-readiness-from-public-distribution-readiness.md))

## North star and segment comparison

- [Accepted intent] The north star is segmented Meeting Asset Workflow Success Rate, with completion through saved Record, reopened Record Review, Smart Minutes review, and export; existing quality, privacy, reliability, analytics, and diagnostics contracts remain guardrails, and measurement evidence is not automatically a threshold. ([Sources: GH-57](https://github.com/YannJY02/AutoTranscribe/issues/57), [analytics contract](../../../analytics/sql/v1/README.md))
- [Accepted intent] The accepted canary scope compares Local and Cloud Smart Minutes parity across Live and Import through Record Review and export for one owner on the reference Mac. ([Sources: GH-56](https://github.com/YannJY02/AutoTranscribe/issues/56))
- [Unknown] **Live/local baseline:** no traceable baseline rate or threshold is stored in the merged evidence; the pilot therefore cannot report a baseline delta. **Pilot:** one synthetic UI-route attempt started and opened Smart Minutes review, but Record save, reopen, and export were unobserved; workflow completion failed, and local analysis mode itself was not observed. ([Sources: live/local proof](../../../pilots/evidence/GH-73/live-local-proof.json), [pilot manifest](../../../pilots/gh-73-owner-lifecycle.json))
- [Unknown] **Live/cloud baseline and pilot:** no traceable baseline rate or threshold is stored, and the provider path was not exercised; every lifecycle stage was unobserved. ([Sources: pilot manifest](../../../pilots/gh-73-owner-lifecycle.json))
- [Unknown] **Import/local baseline and pilot:** no traceable baseline rate or threshold is stored, and the attempt was blocked when the exclusive-macOS window was exhausted; every lifecycle stage was unobserved. ([Sources: pilot manifest](../../../pilots/gh-73-owner-lifecycle.json))
- [Unknown] **Import/cloud baseline and pilot:** no traceable baseline rate or threshold is stored; the attempt was blocked when the exclusive-macOS window was exhausted and the provider path was not exercised; every lifecycle stage was unobserved. ([Sources: pilot manifest](../../../pilots/gh-73-owner-lifecycle.json))
- [Inference] No segment-level success rate, cross-segment parity result, or baseline change can be calculated from one failed attempt and three unobserved attempts. ([Sources: pilot outcome](../../../pilots/evidence/GH-73/pilot-outcome.json), [pilot manifest](../../../pilots/gh-73-owner-lifecycle.json))

## Guardrails, failures, and recovery

- [Observed] The exact pilot build was source-bound, locally distributed, Apple Development signed, and passed the recorded deep-signature and bundle/source-attestation checks; this is local build provenance, not public distribution proof. ([Sources: build proof](../../../pilots/evidence/GH-73/build-proof.json))
- [Observed] The Live/local synthetic route failed after opening Smart Minutes review; Record persistence, reopening, and export were not observed. ([Sources: live/local proof](../../../pilots/evidence/GH-73/live-local-proof.json))
- [Observed] The rollback dry-run verified the frozen app target and passed tests for consent revocation, deterministic queue-purge readback, and Sentry disablement while preserving the evidence hash; it did not act on or read back the current owner runtime. ([Sources: rollback proof](../../../pilots/evidence/GH-73/rollback-proof.json))
- [Observed] The PostHog readback reconciled 14 prerequisite-synthetic aggregate events, including one failure and one successful recovery pair; these were not events from the failed pilot attempt. ([Sources: PostHog readback](../../../pilots/evidence/GH-73/posthog-readback.json), [pilot manifest](../../../pilots/gh-73-owner-lifecycle.json))
- [Observed] The Sentry readback recorded bounded release metadata and a succeeded recovery classification for prerequisite-synthetic evidence; it is not a production incident or pilot-attempt outcome. ([Sources: Sentry readback](../../../pilots/evidence/GH-73/sentry-readback.json), [pilot manifest](../../../pilots/gh-73-owner-lifecycle.json))
- [Observed] The Langfuse readback recorded a four-item metadata dataset experiment with nine scores across failure behavior, latency, and source-evidence linkage; it is SDK-experiment evidence, not an external-user quality result. ([Sources: Langfuse readback](../../../pilots/evidence/GH-73/langfuse-readback.json))
- [Observed] The published pilot artifacts mark their bounded content privacy-safe, and the outcome explicitly makes no production-readiness claim. ([Sources: pilot outcome](../../../pilots/evidence/GH-73/pilot-outcome.json), [evidence ledger](../../../pilots/evidence/GH-73/evidence-ledger.json))
- [Unknown] No observed guardrail threshold result, production telemetry result, external-user validation, raw quality score, or complete four-segment privacy exercise exists in the merged pilot evidence. ([Sources: pilot manifest](../../../pilots/gh-73-owner-lifecycle.json), [pilot outcome](../../../pilots/evidence/GH-73/pilot-outcome.json))
